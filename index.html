Our Story

Where tradition meets
fine taste

For over twenty years, Chaitanya has been the quiet benchmark for culinary craft and warm hospitality along the NH-6 — a place travellers plan their journeys around.

Our kitchen honours heirloom recipes, prepared by seasoned chefs with hand-picked spices and the freshest local produce. Alongside, a considered wine cellar gathers selections from India's finest vineyards and beyond — each chosen to belong at your table.

  • Authentic gastronomy

    Tandoori classics, slow dum biryanis and rich gravies, crafted with precision.

  • A considered cellar

    Cabernet, Shiraz and crisp whites — paired thoughtfully with every course.

  • Calm, private spaces

    Air-conditioned dining halls and intimate family seating for every occasion.

  • Attentive service

    A team devoted to unhurried, memorable evenings — every single day.
